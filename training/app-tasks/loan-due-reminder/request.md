Members keep forgetting to bring tools back. When a member borrows a tool,
email them a reminder at 9:00 in the morning (UTC, the app's time zone) on
the day before the loan is due. Subject: "Reminder: <tool name> is due back
<due date>", with the date written the way the overdue email writes it; the
body must include the tool's name and serial. If 9:00 the day before has
already passed when they borrow, send no reminder. If the tool is returned
before the reminder goes out, don't send it.
The app has no scheduler for recurring work apart from the nightly overdue
sweep at midnight, so the reminder can't come from a new periodic job.
