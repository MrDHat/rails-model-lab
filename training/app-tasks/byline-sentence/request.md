Bylines run the names together with commas ("By Eli Moana, Hana Reti") and
don't follow the order the editor chose in the byline field, which is saved as
each authorship's position. The style guide wants "By Eli Moana and Hana Reti",
and with three or more names "By Eli Moana, Hana Reti and Tom Bell" (no comma
before "and"), in byline order. Fix it everywhere a byline shows: story pages,
the lists of stories, and the `byline` field in the app's API.
