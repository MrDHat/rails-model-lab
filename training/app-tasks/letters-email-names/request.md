The letters emails only use bare addresses. The desk's mail client shows a
writer as just their address, and writers get our acknowledgement from
"letters@gazette.example". Add names to both emails: the desk's copy should
have the writer's name and address in Reply-To (`June Hart
<june.hart@example.com>`), and the acknowledgement should be addressed to the
writer by name and come from "Harbour Gazette letters desk"
<letters@gazette.example>.

Writers sign their letters however they like, so names can contain commas,
quotes and accents ("Hart, June", `Te Rangi "Bub" Wiremu`, "Zoë Māhina").
