Sections appear on the front page in alphabetical order, so Business comes
before News. The chief editor wants to choose the order. Add a "Front page
order" field to the settings page, a comma-separated list of section slugs
such as `news, sport, business`. The front page shows those sections in that
order, then any sections not in the list, alphabetically. Slugs that don't
match a section are ignored, and an empty list means alphabetical order.
